NOOR CLI — Sample Output
========================

Captured on 25 October 2026 (Asia/Dhaka). ANSI colors are omitted here;
the live CLI uses emerald for the next prayer, gold for Hijri dates, and
cyan for the clock and dates. Prayer times in this sample were fetched
from the AlAdhan API — they are not hard-coded in the application.

The default Asr school is Shafi (0), which is why Asr is shown at 03:00 PM.
